# 0006 — One model of the project, and it is the Flow's

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-16, the owner: "melhor juntar já que estão fazendo a mesma coisa"). **Where:** `server/arch/flowmodel.mjs`, `server/foundation/modelview.mjs`, `server/foundation/blocks.mjs`.

**Context.** The program had two models of a project. The Foundation's round wrote `model.json` (a C4 picture made from folders: a system, a container, a few components; 4 nodes and 1 arrow for this repository) and nothing but "Tudo em dia?" read it; its routes were an empty list. The Flow drew the page from a second model (`flow-model.json`: the parts of the map, the actors and the outside services, 23 nodes and 86 arrows, each with the README lines and the code lines it rests on). Two builders, two caches, two answers to "what is in this program".

**Decision.** The Flow's model is the one. The refresh round builds it (the `model` step), so it is fresh in session-map's storage; the page, `GET /api/foundation/<id>/model` and "Tudo em dia?" read that same model. The lines of "Tudo em dia?" that judge the map against the code read it through a small shape made on the spot (`linesModelOf`), never kept. The old builder (`server/foundation/model.mjs`) and the study built on its shape (`enrich.mjs`, `evidence-check.mjs`) are no longer called by the round; they stay in the repository with their tests, and what they were for (the AI's claims over the model) waits with `fl07`.

**Consequences.** One place says what the program is made of. Six of the seven lines that read it (the golden rule and what the readers found: routes, screens, tables, jobs, integrations) went from "warning" to "confirmed", because the old model had no box for most parts; the seventh (the drawn arrows against the real imports) is now a true warning: the README's drawing leaves out 26 strong imports, and two of its arrows have no import behind them. The Flow's builder still has its own scan of the code beside the Foundation's readers: the two readers could be joined one day, behind the same model, without touching what reads it.
