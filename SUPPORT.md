# Getting help

- **A question or something that does not work:** open an [issue](https://github.com/dan-abreu/session-map/issues/new/choose) and pick the template. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened; the log of the day (`~/.claude/session-map/logs/`) helps, and the **Logs** tab of the map reads it in plain words.
- **A security problem:** do not open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **How it works:** the [README](README.md) (also in [Portuguese](README.pt-BR.md)) and the plan in [docs/architecture](docs/architecture/README.md); the words the project uses are in the [glossary](docs/glossary.md).
- **Contributing a change:** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

session-map is made by one person in his own time: there is no support contract and no promised answer time.
